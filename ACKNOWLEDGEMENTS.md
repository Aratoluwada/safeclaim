# Acknowledgements & Data Attributions

SafeClaim AI is developed as part of the **OSCA OPEN//BUILD 2026** Hackathon. We gratefully acknowledge the following regulatory agencies, civil society initiatives, and open-source projects.

---

## 🏛️ Official Regulatory & Public Health Authorities

All factual statements, emergency numbers, and regulatory guidelines indexed in SafeClaim are derived from official public circulars and gazettes published by:

1. **Nigeria Centre for Disease Control and Prevention (NCDC)**
   * Public health advisories on Viral Haemorrhagic Fevers, Ebola virus containment, biosecurity protocols, and the National Emergency Toll-Free Hotline **6232**.
   * Portal: `https://ncdc.gov.ng`

2. **Federal Competition and Consumer Protection Commission (FCCPC)**
   * Limited Interim Regulatory Framework for Digital Money Lending (DMLs), directives prohibiting borrower harassment and contact scraping, and the Consumer Protection Helpline **0800 033 2272**.
   * Portal: `https://fccpc.gov.ng`

3. **National Social Safety Nets Project (NASSP) / Federal Ministry of Humanitarian Affairs**
   * National Social Register (NSR) community enumeration procedures, anti-fraud guidelines for Conditional Cash Transfers (CCT), and the Grievance Redress line **0700 227 4835**.
   * Portal: `https://nassp.gov.ng`

4. **Independent National Electoral Commission (INEC)**
   * Regulations and guidelines on voting procedures, mandatory BVAS accreditation, polling timelines, and the Citizens' Contact Centre line **0700-CALL-INEC**.
   * Portal: `https://inecnigeria.org`

## 🤖 Open-Weight Foundation Model

SafeClaim integrates open-weight models for Natural Language Inference (NLI) and factual verification:
* **Model:** [Qwen/Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct](https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct) (Alibaba Cloud / Qwen Team)
* **License:** [Apache 2.0 License](https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct/blob/main/LICENSE) (Permissive open-weights, commercial and research friendly)
* **Runtime:** Served via OpenAI-compatible API protocol (Ollama / vLLM / Groq).
