# SafeClaim AI — Image Briefs & Art Direction

## Core Design Identity: "The Trusted Neighbour with the Receipts"

SafeClaim AI's visual identity shifts completely away from cold, robotic, synthetic blue tech aesthetics into warm, tactile, civic and physical document metaphors.

### Art Direction Principles
1. **Physical Receipts & Official Seals**: Documents feel like real pulled gazettes, notices, and advisories with postal stamp edges and rubber-stamp ink impressions.
2. **Warm Paper Textures**: Parchment backgrounds (`#FBF7F0`, `#F5EFEB`), deep warm ink (`#1F1B16`), Nigeria forest green (`#0A5C36`), and rich Yoruba Adire indigo (`#24306E`).
3. **Warm Duotone Photography & Editorial Graphics**: Avoid generic stock photos of corporate handshakes or synthetic AI faces. Use authentic community scenes, market conversations, newsroom monitors, and public health posters.
4. **Natural Nigerian Context**: Visuals reflect real communication channels (WhatsApp forwards, radio broadcasts, community notice boards) and official institutions (NCDC, FCCPC, NHIS, INEC, CBN).

### Asset Manifest
- `hero-neighbour.svg`: Two neighbours reviewing an official circular together over tea, with an adire accent strip and rubber stamps.
- `official-archive.svg`: Neatly filed Nigerian federal gazettes, official advisories, and certified registry stamps.
- `newsroom-desk.svg`: The Rumour Desk interface showing synthetic demo datasets, guidance gap alerts, and outbreak monitoring.
- `community-hotline.svg`: Toll-free NCDC 6232 public helpline badge with free-of-charge rubber stamp.
- `og-card.svg`: High-resolution OpenGraph sharing card with the 3 verdict stamps.
