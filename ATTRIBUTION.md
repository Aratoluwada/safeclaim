# SafeClaim AI — Asset & Source Attributions

## Official Public Domain & Government Portals
All citations, circular excerpts, and official gazettes referenced in SafeClaim AI are derived from official public notices issued by Nigerian federal regulatory and public-service bodies:
- **NCDC (Nigeria Centre for Disease Control & Prevention)**: Toll-free public health hotline `6232`, WhatsApp `08099555577`, official advisories on Cholera, Ebola, Lassa Fever, Mpox, and COVID-19. Website: `https://ncdc.gov.ng`.
- **FCCPC (Federal Competition & Consumer Protection Commission)**: Consumer advisories on delisted predatory loan apps and deceptive health remedy marketing. Website: `https://fccpc.gov.ng`.
- **NHIA / NHIS (National Health Insurance Authority)**: Official guidance on citizen enrolment and accredited healthcare providers. Website: `https://nhia.gov.ng`.
- **INEC (Independent National Electoral Commission)**: Official gazettes on voter registration and polling unit procedures. Website: `https://inec.gov.ng`.
- **CBN (Central Bank of Nigeria)**: Official regulatory directives on currency circulation, banking fraud, and consumer protection. Website: `https://cbn.gov.ng`.
