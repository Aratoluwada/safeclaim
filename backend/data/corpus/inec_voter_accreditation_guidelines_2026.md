### 1. Mandatory Accreditation Through BVAS
In accordance with the Electoral Act, accreditation of voters using the Bimodal Voter Accreditation System (BVAS) is mandatory for every election conducted by the Independent National Electoral Commission (INEC).
- A voter must physically present an authentic Permanent Voter Card (PVC) issued by INEC at the specific polling unit where they are registered.
- Accreditation is confirmed solely by matching fingerprint biometric data or facial recognition on the BVAS device against the registered voter database.
- Manual accreditation, incident forms, and voting by proxy are strictly abolished and illegal under current electoral guidelines. If BVAS cannot authenticate a voter either through fingerprint or facial recognition, that voter cannot vote.

### 2. Polling Unit Operations and Timelines
- Official voting hours at all polling units nationwide commence at 8:30 AM and conclude at 2:30 PM.
- Every eligible voter already standing in line at or before 2:30 PM must be accredited and allowed to cast their ballot. Polling unit officials may not close queues prematurely.
- Accreditation and voting take place simultaneously.
- Voting is entirely free. No election official or community representative may demand payments or administrative dues from voters.

### 3. Neutrality and Official INEC Information Channels
INEC maintains strict political neutrality. Unofficial online claims alleging cancellation of voting, sudden polling unit relocation, or online electronic ballot submission via third-party web apps are false. All verified operational announcements are transmitted only via:
