### 1. Regulatory Status of Digital Money Lenders (DMLs)
The Federal Competition and Consumer Protection Commission (FCCPC), in conjunction with the Joint Regulatory and Enforcement Task Force (JRETF), reminds members of the public that only Digital Money Lenders (DMLs) registered under the Limited Interim Regulatory Framework are authorized to offer online credit services in Nigeria. Unregistered lending applications operating through direct APK distribution, third-party social media download links, or unverified app marketplaces are strictly illegal.

### 2. Prohibited Debt Recovery Methods and Harassment
Under Sections 17 and 18 of the FCCPA (2018), digital lenders are strictly prohibited from engaging in abusive, coercive, or defamatory recovery practices. Specifically:
- Accessing or scraping borrower contact lists, personal photos, or storage without explicit, verifiable consent is unlawful.
- Sending mass defamation text messages, funeral posters, or threatening messages to contacts, employers, or relatives of borrowers is illegal and constitutes criminal cyber-harassment.
- Unilateral imposition of interest rates exceeding approved ceilings or arbitrary processing penalties is illegal.
- Licensed financial institutions and authorized DMLs will never threaten physical harm or publish personal data on public online forums.

### 3. Red Flags of Fake and Fraudulent Loan Applications
Consumers should remain vigilant against predatory loan operators exhibiting the following characteristics:
- Requiring upfront registration fees, administrative deposits, or processing clearing fees before loans are disbursed.
- Distributing application installation files (.apk) directly via WhatsApp, Telegram, or unsolicited SMS messages rather than recognized platforms.
- Demanding access to device contacts, media libraries, and call logs as an mandatory prerequisite for microloans.
- Falsely claiming partnership with the Central Bank of Nigeria (CBN) or Federal Government welfare schemes.

### 4. How to Verify Approved Lenders and File Complaints
Consumers who experience harassment, defamation, or unauthorized account deductions from digital lenders are urged to report immediately:
- Check the official whitelist of approved and conditionally approved digital lenders at: https://fccpc.gov.ng/approved-dmls
- Official FCCPC Consumer Protection Hotline: 0800 033 2272 (Toll-Free, Monday – Friday 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM)
- Dedicated DML Harassment Reporting Desk: dmlcompliance@fccpc.gov.ng
- Official Online Complaint Form: https://fccpc.gov.ng/complaints
