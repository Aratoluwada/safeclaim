### 1. Official National Social Register (NSR) Enrollment Procedure
The National Social Safety Nets Project (NASSP) and the Federal Ministry of Humanitarian Affairs maintain the verified National Social Register (NSR) of poor and vulnerable households. 
- Enrollment in the National Social Register is strictly community-based and conducted in person through trained local government enumerators using official electronic data capture devices.
- Citizens cannot be registered into the social register via unsolicited social media links, text messages, or third-party web portals.
- All beneficiary identities are validated using the National Identity Number (NIN) and Bank Verification Number (BVN) to ensure direct, verifiable disbursement.

### 2. Safeguards Against Fake Grant Schemes and Fraud
Members of the public are cautioned against widespread fraudulent messages circulating on WhatsApp, Facebook, and SMS claiming to offer federal grants or cash transfers:
- The Federal Government never charges any application fee, registration fee, card issuance fee, or validation payment to receive social assistance or palliative transfers. Any portal requesting money to release funds is fraudulent.
- Official disbursements are transferred directly into verified personal bank accounts or authorized mobile money wallets linked to the beneficiary's NIN. Cash handovers by political intermediaries are prohibited.
- The government does not require beneficiaries to share card PINs, CVV codes, or one-time passwords (OTPs) under any circumstance.
